## Ingredients

- Handful | Arugula/Spinach/Spring Mix |
- 2.5 oz | Whole Grain Spagetti/Pasta |
- 3 oz | Chicken Breast |
- 2 tsp | Canola/Olive/Coconut Oil |
- 1.5 tbsp | Parmesan/Cheddar Cheese |

## Directions

1. Cook pasta to package
2. Heat oil in skillet on medium-high heat
3. Season meat with S&P and brown on each side
4. Add cooked pasta and cheese, stir to coat & turn off heat
5. Add washed and drained greens and enjoy!
