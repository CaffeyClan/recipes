## Ingredients

-  | 1 cup Sugar | 
-  | 1 stick Butter, softened | 
-  | 1 Egg | 
-  | 1 cup Peanut Butter | 
-  | 1 tsp Vanilla | 
-  | 1/2 tsp Salt | 
-  | 1/2 tsp Baking Soda | 
-  | 1 1/2 cup Flour | 

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Mix ingredients one at a time starting at top
3. Roll into 1 inch balls
4. Roll in sugar and flatten with fork for criss-cross design OR shape to small patty to top with hershey kiss after baking
5. Bake 12-15 minutes
