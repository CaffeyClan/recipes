## Ingredients

-  | 3 cups Chocolate Chips | 
-  | 1 can Sweetened Condensed Milk | 
-  | Dash of Salt | 
-  | 1/2 - 1 cup Chopped Nuts | 
-  | 1 1/2 tsp Vanilla | 

## Directions

1. In saucepan, on low heat melt chips, milk, and salt. Stir constantly
2. Remove from heat, add nuts and vanilla
3. Spread evenly in square pan lined with wax paper
4. Chill for 2 hours, or until firm
