## Ingredients

- 4 ounces | Chicken | 
- 2 ounces | Whole-grain spaghetti | 
- 1 tsp | Olive oil | 
- 2 tbsp | Parmesan | 
- 2 tbsp | Mozzarella | 

## Directions

1. Cook pasta
2. Cut chicken into bit size pieces
3. With oil, fry until golden brown
4. Add chicken and cheese to pasta
