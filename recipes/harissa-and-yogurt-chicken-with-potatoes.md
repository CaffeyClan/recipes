## Ingredients

- 6 sprays | Cooking spray | 
- 1 pound | Baby potatoes | 
- 3/4 tsp | Salt | 
- 1 cup | Plain low fat yogurt | 
- 1/3 cup | Peppermint leaves | 
- 2 tsp | Ground cumin | 
- 1 tsp | Harissa paste | 
- 2 1/2 pounds | Chicken | 

## Directions

1. Preheat the oven to 450°F. Spray large sheet pan with cooking spray.
2. Add potatoes to pan, coat with cooking spray and season with salt.
3. Roast for 15 minutes.
4. In large bowl, combine the yogurt, mint, cumin, harissa, and 1/2 tsp salt.
5. Add chicken and coat.
6. Reduce oven temp to 400°F. Stir the potatoes, ppush to side of pan.
7. Arrange chicken on pan, spoon remaining sauce on top.
8. Roast until cooked, about 30-35 minutes.
