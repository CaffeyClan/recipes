## Ingredients

- 4 tbsp | Rice wild/basmati/brown |
- 4.5 oz | Chicken breast |
- ½ cup | Cauliflower/Broccoli/Brussel sprouts |
- ½ cup | Squash Pumpkin/Butternut |
- 3 tsp | Olive oil/Sesame oil |
-  | S&P |
- ⅓ tsp | Dried Basil |

## Directions

1. Cook rice according to package
2. Preheat oven to 430
3. Season ckn with S&P
4. Place ckn and chopped veg on pan
5. Drizzle with oil, Cook 15-20 min
6. Season, serve rice and pan, enjoy!
