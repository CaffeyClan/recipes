## Ingredients

- 1/2 tbsp | Coconut/Canola/Olive Oil |
- 1/2 cup | Kidney/Navy/Black Beans |
- 3 oz | Ground Turkey |
- 1 | Tomato/Bell Pepper |
- 4 tbsp | Millet/Quinoa/Buckwheat |
-  | Garlic, 1 clove, minced |
- Pinch | Chili Powder |
- Pinch | Oregano |
-  | S&P |

## Directions

1. Heat oil in pot over medium heat
2. Add garlic, cook ~ 30 seconds
3. Add meat and cook until done
4. Add chopped veggies and seasonings
5. Add beans, grain, and water *double the grain amount*
6. Mix well and bring to boil
7. Season with salt, cover and simmer on low ~20 minutes
8. Season with desired seasonings and enjoy!
