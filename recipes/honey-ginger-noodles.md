## Ingredients

-  | 8 ounces Cooked Noodles | 
-  | 2 tbsp Oil | 
-  | 2 tbsp Honey | 
-  | 2 tbsp Soy Sauce | 
-  | 1 tsp Sesame Seeds | 
-  | 1 tsp Ground Ginger | 
-  | 1/2 tsp Red Pepper Flakes | 
-  | 1/2 tsp Pepper | 
-  | Green Onion for topping | 

## Directions

1. Mix Honey-Pepper, set aside
2. Add oil on medium-high heat
3. Add noodles and cook for 2 minutes
4. Add mixture, cook 5 minutes on medium–low
5. Top with diced green onions
