## Ingredients

-  | Potatoes: | 
-  | 7 medium Russet Potatoes | 
-  | 1 cup Chicken Stock | 
-  | 1 cup Milk | 
-  | 2 tsp Garlic Powder | 
-  | S & P | 
-  | Sauce: | 
-  | 1/2 cup Ketchup | 
-  | 2 tbsp Brown Sugar | 
-  | Meatloaf: | 
-  | 2 pounds lean Ground Beef | 
-  | 1 cup Bread Crumbs | 
-  | 2 Eggs | 
-  | 1/4 cup Minced Onion | 
-  | 2 tsp Garlic Powder | 
-  | 1 tsp Parsley | 
-  | 1/2 cup BBQ Sauce | 
-  | S & P | 

## Directions

1. - Add meatloaf to top of potatoes
2. - Seal & pressure cook for 25 minutes. Let naturally release for 10 minutes.
3. -Once done, open and remove meatloaf, top with sauce.
4. -Mash the potatoes and add remaining seasonings to them.
5. Meatloaf:
6. Combine ingredients in bowl.
7. Place meat on top of foil and shape into loaf.
8. Bring foil sides up around loaf
9. Potatoes:
10. Peel & dice potatoes into cubes
11. Place in Instant Pot and add 1 cup chicken broth
12. Sauce:
13. Mix all ingredients, Set aside.
