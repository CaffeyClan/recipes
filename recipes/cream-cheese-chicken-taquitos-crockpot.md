## Ingredients

- 2 | Chicken Breasts |
- 1 tsp | Chili Powder |
- 1 tsp | Garlic Powder |
- 1 tsp | Cumin |
-  | S&P |
- 8 ounces | Cream Cheese |
- 1/3 cup | Water |
- 1/2 cup | Mexican or Colby Jack Cheese |
-  | Tortillas of choice |

## Directions

1. Add chicken - water to crockpot
2. Cover and cook low for 8 hours or high for 4 hours
3. Shred and stir chicken
4. Preheat oven 400
5. Make taquitos by adding chicken and cheese to tortilla and rolling up snug
6. Place taquitos on baking sheet
7. Bake 10 minutes
8. Serve with salsa, guacamole, or queso.
9. Eat with Cheesy Tortilla Soup.
