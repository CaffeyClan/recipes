## Ingredients

-  | 1 tbsp Butter | 
-  | 6 oz Dry Whole Wheat Orzo | 
-  | 2 cloves Garlic, minced | 
-  | 2 cups Chicken Broth | 
-  | 1/4 cup Water | 
-  | 4 oz Broccoli, finely chopped | 
-  | 2 oz Sharp Cheddar, shredded | 
-  | 1/4 tsp Mustard Powder | 
-  | 1 tsp Parsley, chopped | 
-  | 1/4 tsp Salt | 
-  | Pepper, to taste | 

## Directions

1. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add butter until melted, then add Orzo. Cook ~3 minutes, until brown
2. Add garlic and cook together, ~1 minute
3. Add broth, bring to boil. Cover and simmer 5 minutes.
4. Add broccoli, cover and simmer 10 minutes.
5. Remove from heat, add remaining cheese and remaining seasonings.
