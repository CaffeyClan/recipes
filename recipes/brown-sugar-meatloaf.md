## Ingredients

-  | 1/2 cup Brown Sugar | 
-  | 1/2 cup Ketchup | 
-  | 1 1/2 pounds Ground Beef | 
-  | 1/2 cup Milk | 
-  | 2 Eggs | 
-  | 1 1/2 tsp Salt | 
-  | 1/4 tsp Black Pepper | 
-  | 1 Onion, grated | 
-  | 1/4 tsp Ground Ginger | 
-  | 3/4 cup Crushed Crackers | 

## Directions

1. Preheat oven 350
2. Mix ingredients all together well
3. Shape to loaf, put in pan with eging to catch drippings
4. Cook 1 hour
