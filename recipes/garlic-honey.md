## Ingredients

- 1/4 cup | raw honey |

## Directions

1. Finely grate raw garlic and let sit for 10 minutes to activate medicinal compounds.
2. Add honey to garlic and mix.
3. Can be consumed immediately.
4. Store in a sealed, sterilized, and dry jar.
5. Stir well with dry clean spoon and turn jar to mix daily and before taking.
6. For internal use, spread 1 tablespoon on a piece of bread and eat, twice a day during active infection.
