## Ingredients

- 3 cups | strong freshly brewed hot Coffee |
- 3 tbsp | Chocolate Syrup |
- 1 tsp | Sugar |
- 1/3 cup | Heavy Cream |
- 1/4 cup | Creme de Cacao or Irish Cream *Optional |
-  | Whipped Cream *Optional |
-  | Chocolate shavings *Optional |

## Directions

1. Combine coffee, syrup, and sugar in crockpot. Cover and cook low 2 hours.
2. Stir in heavy cream and creme de cacao, Cover and cook additional 30 minutes
3. Enjoy, adding toppings of choice.
