## Ingredients

-  | 1/2 cup Melted Butter | 
-  | 2 Eggs | 
-  | 1 box Jiffy Cornbread Mix | 
-  | 1 can Whole Kernel Corn, drained | 
-  | 1 can Cream Corn | 
-  | 1 cup Sour Cream | 

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Mix all ingredients together until well combined.
3. Add to baking pan
4. Bake at 350 for 45 minutes or until golden brown
