## Ingredients

- 2.5 tbsp | Quinoa |
- 2.5 tbsp | Lentil |
- 1.5 oz | Tofu |
- 1/2 tbsp | Oil |
- 1 tbsp | Lemon Juice |
- 1 tsp | Dijon Mustard |
- 1 tbsp | Parsely |

## Directions

1. Rinse & cook lentils ~20 min/tender. Drain & Set aside
2. Cook quinoa to pkg. Let cool
3. Dressing: whisk oil, lemon juice, Dijon mustard, S&P
4. Add lentils, quinoa, parsely, tofu. Top with dressing
