## Ingredients

- 4 oz | Chicken Breast |
- 3 tsp | Sesame Oil/ Olive Oil |
- 4 tbsp | Rice Wild/Basmati/Brown |
- ½ medium | Tomato/Bell pepper |
- ½ cup | Cauliflower/Broccoli/Brussel sprouts |

## Directions

1. Cook grain according to pkg
2. Preheat broiler
3. Season ckn and veg with S&P
4. Cook 6-8 minutes (til ckn cooked)
5. Serve rice, ckn, and veg!
