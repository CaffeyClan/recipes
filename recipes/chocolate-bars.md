## Ingredients

-  | Crust: | 
-  | 1 1/2 cup Graham Cracker Crumbs | 
-  | 1/2 cup Melted Butter | 
-  | Filling: | 
-  | 1 can Sweetened Condensed Milk | 
-  | 2 cups Chocolate Chips | 
-  | Toppings of choice (nuts, coconut flakes, etc) | 

## Directions

1. Preheat oven 350
2. Mix crust ingredients and press on pan, forming even-later base
3. Pour milk, add chocolate, then evenly layer desired toppings
4. Bake 25 minutes, or until lightly browned
