## Ingredients

-  | 5 Eggs | 
-  | 1/2 cup Sugar | 
-  | 1/2 tsp Salt | 
-  | 3/4 cup Flour | 
-  | 1 can Evaporated Milk | 
-  | 1 tsp Vanilla | 
-  | 16-ounces Frozen Sweet Cherries, thawed | 
-  | Whipped Cream *Optional | 
-  | Cherry-Vanilla Ice Cream *Optional | 

## Directions

1. Spray crockpot with nonstick spray
2. Beat eggs, sugar, & salt until thick and pale yellow
3. Add flour, beat until smooth. Add milk and vanilla, beat until smooth.
4. Pour into crockpot, place cherries on top.
5. Cover, cook low 4 hours or until flan is set.
6. Serve warm with whipped cream and/or ice cream.
