## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup | Cooked chicken breast pieces | 
- 1 tbsp | Light Caesar dressing | 
- 1 tbsp | Plain fat free Greek yogurt | 
- 1 tbsp | Dijon mustard | 
- 2 tsp | Lemon juice | 
- 1/2 tsp | Garlic powder | 
- 2 pinches | Salt | 
- 1 pinch | BBlack pepper | 
- 2 tbsp | Sliced almonds | 
- 1 wrap | Whole wheat wrap | 
- Shredded, 1 cup | Romaine lettuce | 

## Directions

1. In medium bowl, stir together the chicken, dressing, yogurt, mustard, lemon juice, garlic, salt, and pepper.
2. Fold in the almonds.
3. Arrange the chicken salad down the wrap, top with lettuce.
