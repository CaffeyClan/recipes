## Ingredients

-  | 6 cups cooked Elbow Macaroni | 
-  | 2 tbsp Butter | 
-  | 4 cups Evaporated Milk | 
-  | 6 cups Cheddar Cheese, shredded | 
-  | 2 tsp Salt | 
-  | 1/2 tsp Black Pepper | 

## Directions

1. In large bowl, toss macaroni with butter. Stir in milk, cheese, S&P.
2. Add to crockpot, cover and cook high 2-3 hours
