## Ingredients

-  | 4 Apples | 
-  | 2 tsp Cinnamon | 
-  | 2 tsp Sugar | 
-  | Cooking Spray | 

## Directions

1. Preheat oven 200. Spray baking sheet with cooking spray
2. Thinly slice apples
3. Coat apples with cinnamon and sugar
4. Bake 2-3 hours
5. Keep in airtight container, lasts up to 4 days
