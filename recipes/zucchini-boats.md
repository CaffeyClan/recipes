## Ingredients

-  | Large Zucchini | 
-  | Ground Beef | 
-  | Onion | 
-  | Mushroom | 
-  | Bell Pepper | 
-  | Taco Seasoning | 

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 350. On baking sheet, add foil and cooking spray.
2. Prep the zucchini by cutting the ends off, slice in half long-ways, scoop pulp from center and save it.
3. Prep veggies by dicing them into small cubes.
4. In skillet, cook ground beef and drain.
5. Add pulp and diced veggies, saute until veggies soften.
6. Add desired taco seasoning amount. Cook ~2 minutes, remove from heat.
7. Put zucchini boats on foiled and sprayed baking sheet. Fill boats with beef mix. Top with desired cheese.
8. Bake 30 minutes
