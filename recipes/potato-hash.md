## Ingredients

-  | 1 Russet Potato | 
-  | 1 Sweet Potato | 
-  | 3 tbsp Butter | 
-  | 1 cup Bell Pepper, chopped | 
-  | 1 cup Squash, chopped | 
-  | 1 cup Zucchini, chopped | 
-  | 1 cup Onion, chopped | 
-  | S&P | 

## Directions

1. Poke & Microwave potatoes until soft, then chop
2. With skillet on medium heat, add butter
3. Add remaining ingredients and cook
4. Add potatoes last, once other veggies are cooked
5. Cook for 5-10 minutes
