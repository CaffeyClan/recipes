## Ingredients

- 1 cup | Mixed Berries | 
- 1 tsp | Honey | 
- 1/2 tsp | Cinnamon | 
- 2 scoops | Whey protein powder | about 60 grams
- 1/4 cup | Mixed nuts | 
- 1 tsp | Chia seeds | optional

## Directions

1. In a bowl, combine berries, honey, and cinnamon.
2. Stir in protein powder- add a splash of milk/water to help mix smoothly.
3. Top with mixed nuts and optional chia seeds
4. Enjoy immediately!
