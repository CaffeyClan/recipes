## Ingredients

-  | 1 tbsp Crushed Red Pepper Flakes | 
-  | 1 tbsp Black Pepper | 
-  | 1 tbsp Oregano | 
-  | 1 tbsp Basil | 
-  | 1 tbsp Parsley | 
-  | 1 ½ tsp Garlic Powder | 
-  | 1 ½ tsp Onion Powder | 
-  | 1 ½ tsp Salt | 
-  | ½ tsp Rosemary | 
-  | 3 cloves minced Garlic | 
-  | Olive Oil | 

## Directions

1. Mix all the seasonings together (red pepper - garlic)
2. Add 1 tbsp of seasoning to dipping bowl
3. Add olive oil to bowl to desired amount
4. Dip bread and enjoy!
