## Ingredients

-  | 5 pounds Potatoes | 
-  | 2 sticks Butter | 
-  | 1 8-ounce Cream Cheese | 
-  | 1 1/2 tsp Salt | 
-  | 1 tsp Black Pepper | 

## Directions

1. Boil Potatoes in water until soft.
2. Drain
3. Mash & mix in remaining ingredients
