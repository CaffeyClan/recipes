## Ingredients

- 100g | Rice cakes | 
- 250g | Chicken breast | 
- 1 tbsp | Brown sugar | 
- 2 tbsp | Gochugaru | 
- 1 tbsp | Goshujang | 
- 2 tsp | Black pepper | 
- 1 tbsp | Soy sauce | 
- 1/2 tbsp | Sesame oil | 
- 1 tbsp | Curry powder | 
- 2 tsp, minced | Garlic | 
- 1 tbsp | Mirin | 
- 100g | Nappa cabbage | 
- 1 cup | Red onion | 
- 1/2 cup | Green onions | 
- 1 tsp | Sesame seeds | 

## Directions

1. Dice nappa cabbage, green onions, and red onions.
2. Cut chicken into bit-size slices, mix with brown sugar, gochugaru, black pepper, soy sauce, sesame oil, gochujang, mirin, a curry cube, and minced garlic.
3. In a pan, spray cooking oil and heat to medium-low heat, cook chicken until cooked through.
4. Add red onions, nappa cabbage, green onions, and rice cakes. Stir-fry until everything is evenly marinated.
5. Top with sesame seeds and enjoy!
