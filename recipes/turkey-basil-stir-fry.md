## Ingredients

-  | 4 ounces Rice noodles | 
-  | 3/4 cup Chicken Broth | 
-  | 3 tbsp Soy Sauce | 
-  | 1 tbsp Cornstarch | 
-  | 1 pound Ground Turkey | 
-  | 1/4 tsp Salt | 
-  | 4 tsp Canola Oil | 
-  | 3 Garlic Cloves, minced | 
-  | 4 tsp Ginger, minced | 
-  | 1/4 tsp Red Pepper flakes | 
-  | 4 cups Broccoli Florets | 
-  | 2 Orange Bell Peppers, thinly sliced | 
-  | 4 Scallions, thinly sliced | 
-  | 1/4 cup Fresh Basil, chopped | 

## Directions

1. Cook noodles and drain according to package
2. In small bowl, mix broth, soy sauce, and cornstarch
3. Heat wok on medium-high heat, add oil, turkey, and salt
4. Transfer turkey to plate, add remaining ingredients except basil to wok. Cook until fragrant and al dente.
5. Add turkey, noodles to wok until heated. Whisk broth once more, than add to wok and bring to boil until thickened, about 1 minute
6. Remove from heat and top with basil.
