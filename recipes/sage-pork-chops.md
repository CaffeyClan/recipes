## Ingredients

-  | 4 pork chops, cleaned | 
-  | 1 1/2 tsp Rubbed Sage | 
-  | 1 tsp Minced Garlic (dry is best!) | 
-  | 1 tsp Thyme Leaves | 
-  | 1/2 tsp Ground Allspice | 
-  | 1/2 tsp Paprika | 

## Directions

1. Mix seasonings together
2. Rub on pork chops
3. Cook on skillet or in oven
