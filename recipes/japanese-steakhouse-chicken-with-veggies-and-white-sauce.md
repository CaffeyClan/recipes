## Ingredients

-  | White Sauce | 
-  | 1 1/4 cup Mayo | 
-  | 1 tbsp Melted Butter | 
-  | 1/4 cup water | 
-  | 1 tsp Tomato Paste | 
-  | 1/2 tsp Garlic Powder | 
-  | 1 tsp Sugar | 
-  | 1/4 tsp Paprika | 
-  | 1/4 tsp Cayenne Pepper | 
-  | Chicken & Veggies: | 
-  | Salt to taste | 
-  | Lemon Pepper to taste | 
-  | Soy Sauce to taste | 
-  | Oil to fry Chicken | 
-  | Chicken & Veggies of choice | 

## Directions

1. White Sauce
2. Mix all ingredients for sauce, set in fridge.
3. Chicken & Veggies
4. Season chicken & veggies of choice, then cook in small amount of oil until fully cooked.
5. Typical Preparation:Chicken in bite-size pieces. Veggies usually broccoli and zucchini but can be anything.
