## Ingredients

-  | 1 pound Beef Stew Meat | 
-  | 1 can Cream of Mushroom | 
-  | 8-ounces Mushrooms | 
-  | 1 pkg Onion Soup Mix | 
-  | Egg Noodles, cooked | 

## Directions

1. Combine everything but noodles into crockpot, stir.
2. Cover, cook low 8-10 hours
3. Serve over noodles
