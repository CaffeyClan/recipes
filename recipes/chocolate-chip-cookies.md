## Ingredients

-  | 1 cup Flour | 
-  | 1/2 tsp Baking Soda | 
-  | 1/2 tsp Salt | 
-  | 1 cup Chocolate Chips | 
-  | 1/2 cup Butter, softened | 
-  | 1/2 cup Sugar | 
-  | 1/4 cup Brown Sugar | 
-  | 1 Egg | 
-  | 1 tsp Vanilla | 

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 375
2. In one bowl. Mix dry ingredients (flour, soda, salt)
3. In seperate, bigger bowl mix wet ingredients (remaining ingredients)
4. Slowly add dry mix to wet mix, combining well
5. On baking sheet, drop 1 tbsp balls at 1.5 inches apart
6. Bake 12-14 minutes
