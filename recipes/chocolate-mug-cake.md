## Ingredients

- 3 tbsp | Flour |
- 2 tbsp | Sugar |
- 1 tbsp | Cocoa powder - *Can replace with PB2 Protein Powder |
- 1/4 tsp | Baking Powder |
- Dash | Salt |
- 3 tbsp | Milk |
- 1 tbsp | Canola Oil |
- 1/8 tsp | Vanilla Extract |
- 1 tsp | Chocolate Chips |

## Directions

1. Add ingredients from top to bottom into mug of choice
2. Mix well
3. Microwave for 70-90 seconds

## Family Notes

- Alternate version: Use 3 tablespoons flour, 3 tablespoons sugar, 2 tablespoons cocoa powder, 1/4 teaspoon baking powder, a dash of salt, 3 tablespoons milk, a splash of vanilla, and 3 tablespoons chocolate chips.
