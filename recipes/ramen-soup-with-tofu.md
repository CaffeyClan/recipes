## Ingredients

- 2 cups | Vegetable broth | 
- 1/2 cup, sliced | Shiitake mushrooms | 
- 1 tsp, chopped | Ginger | 
- 1 1/2 oz | Ramen noodles | 
- 4 oz | Firm tofu | 
- 1 medium, thinly sliced | Scallions | 
- 1 tsp | Soy sauce | 

## Directions

1. Bring broth or soup base and mushrooms to boil in medium saucepan.
2. Reduce heat and simmer until mushrooms are tender, about 5 minutes.
3. Stir in ginger. Add ramen noodles and tofu; simmer until noodles soften, about 3 minutes.
4. Pour into bowl and sprinkle with scallion. Serve with soy sauce, if desired.
