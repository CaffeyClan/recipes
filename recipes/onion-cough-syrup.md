## Ingredients

- 2–3 | onions |
-  | White or brown sugar |
-  | Sterilized, dry glass jar and lid |

## Directions

1. Peel and chop the onions.
2. Alternate ½ inch layers of sugar and onion in a glass jar, starting and ending with sugar.
3. Refrigerate overnight.
4. You can strain out the onions or leave them in the syrup.
5. Keep refrigerated.
