## Ingredients

-  | 2 tsp Canola Oil | 
-  | 3/4 pound Ground Turkey | 
-  | 1 Red Onion, diced | 
-  | 3 Garlic Cloves, minced | 
-  | 1 1/4 tsp Ground Cumin | 
-  | 1 tsp Paprika | 
-  | 1/2 tsp Salt | 
-  | Small Pinch Cinnamon | 
-  | 8-ounce can Tomato Sauce | 
-  | 4 Whole Wheat Pita Breads | 
-  | 1 Tomato, diced | 
-  | 1 cup Cilantro, chopped | 
-  | Mozzarella, shredded | 

## Directions

1. Preheat oven 475, spray 2 baking sheets
2. Heat oil in skillet on medium heat. Add turkey and onion until done, ~6 minutes.
3. Add garlic - cinnamon, cook ~1 minute.
4. Stir in tomato sauce, cook ~2 minutes. TUrn off heat.
5. Place pita bread on baking sheets, top with turkey mixture.
6. Bake 8 minutes.
7. Top with tomato, cilantro, and mozzarella
