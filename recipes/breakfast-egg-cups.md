## Ingredients

- 6 | Eggs |
- 1/4 cup | Milk |
- 1/8 tsp | S&P |
- 3/4 cup | Spinach, stack, roll & sliced |
- 1/4 | Shredded Cheddar Cheese |

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 375, spray Muffin Pan
2. Mix all ingredients together
3. Fill each muffin cup to 3/4 full
4. Bake at 375 for 20-25 minutes
5. Storage: Freeze once cooled. To eat, microwave for 1 minute
