## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp | Butter |
- 1/4 cup | Tomato, chopped |
- 1/4 cup | Onion, chopped |
- 1/4 cup | Bell Pepper, chopped |
- 4 | Eggs |
- 2 tbsp | Parsley, chopped |
- 6 tbsp | Sour Cream |
- 1 tbsp | Dijon Mustard |
-  | S&P |

## Directions

1. Melt butter in skillet over medium heat
2. Add tomato-bell pepper, cook until tender, ~3 minutes
3. Mix eggs-S&P in separate bowl with whisk
4. Add egg mixture to skillet, cook until eggs done, ~2 minutes.
