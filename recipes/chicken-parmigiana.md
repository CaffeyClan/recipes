## Ingredients

- 3 sprays | Olive oil cooking spray | 
- 1 pound | Chicken | 
- 2 eggs | Egg whites | 
- 1/2 cup | Breadcrumbs | 
- 1 tbsp | Italian seasoning | 
- 1/2 tsp | Salt | 
- 1 tsp | Olive oil | 
- 1 1/2 cups | Tomato sauce | 
- 1/2 cup | Mozzarella | 
- 2 tbsp | Parmesan | 
- 1 tbsp | Basil | 

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Coat large baking pan with cooking spray.
2. Place chicken between two sheets of wax paper; using a rolling pin, pound the chicken to 1/4-inch thickness.
3. Place the egg whites in a shallow bowl. In another shallow bowl, combine the bread crumbs with Italian seasoning and salt. Dip the chicken in the egg whites, coating it. Then dip the chicken in the bread crumb mixture, coating well.
4. Coat large skillet with cooking spray and add oil; heat over medium-high heat. When hot, add the chicken and cook until lightly browned and no longer pink, about 4 minutes per side.
5. Pour 1/2 cup of sauce into baking dish, add chicken in a single layer, pour remaining sauce over chiekn, sprinkle with cheeses.
6. Bake for 25 minutes, garnish with basil
