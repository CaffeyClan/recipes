## Ingredients

-  | 3 quarts Water | 
-  | 1 pound Broccolini | 
-  | 3 Bacon Slices | 
-  | 1/2 cup Sweet Onion, diced | 
-  | 1/4 cup Chicken Broth | 
-  | S&P | 

## Directions

1. Bring water to boil, add broccolini and bring to boil, cook for 4 minutes and drain
2. In skillet, cook bacon until done, remove and crumble bacon.
3. In bacon skillet, ass onion and saute 4 minutes, add S&P
4. Add broccolini to skillet, turn off heat, and top with bacon crumbles.
