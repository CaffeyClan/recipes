## Ingredients

- 5.5 tbsp | Red/White Quinoa |
- 1.5 tbsp | Hummus |
- 3 oz | Chicken Breast |
- Handful | Spinach/Arugula/Spring Mix |
- 1 tsp | Olive/Canola/Sunflower Oil |
-  | S&P |

## Directions

1. Cook grain according to package, cooling afterwards
2. Heat oil in skillet over medium heat
3. Add chicken and S&P, frying until golden brown, turn heat off
4. In bowl, add greens, grain, hummus, and chicken
5. Enjoy!
