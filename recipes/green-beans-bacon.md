## Ingredients

-  | 2 pounds Fresh Green Beans | 
-  | 8 strips Bacon, chopped | 
-  | 3 tbsp Butter | 
-  | S & P | 
-  | Diced potatoes | 
-  | Diced White Onion | 
-  | Minced Garlic | 

## Directions

1. Steam green beans until soft
2. Cook bacon
3. Add all ingredients to pot, cook on medium-high heat until warmed.
4. This recipe calls for all ingredients to be cooked separately, then combined and heated at the end.
