## Ingredients

- 2 | Bell Peppers (Red, Green, Yellow) |
- 2 tbsp | White/Red Onion/4.5 tbsp Scallions |
- 2 tbsp | Sweet Chili Sauce/Marinara/Tomato Sauce |
- 6 tbsp | Wild Rice/Brown Rice/White Quinoa |

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 350; Cook grain to package directions
2. Trim top and scoop out bell peppers
3. Mix grain, chopped veggies, and sauce together
4. Fill pepper with mix and bake about 15 minutes
