## Ingredients

-  | Fresh Asparagus | 
-  | 2 tbsp Olive Oil | 
-  | 1/2 tsp Salt | 
-  | 1/2 tsp Oregano, dired | 
-  | 2 tbsp Parmesan Cheese, grated | 

## Directions

1. Preheat oven 425. Spray baking sheet with cooking spray
2. Mix oil, salt, and oregano in large bowl. Wash asparagus and dry.
3. Toss asparagus into oil mix and coat well
4. Lay flat on baking sheet, bake 10 minutes
5. Top with parmesan cheese, bake additional 5-8 minutes.
