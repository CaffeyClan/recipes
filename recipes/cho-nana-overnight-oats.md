## Ingredients

- 1 medium | Banana | 
- 1/3 cup | Rolled oats | 
- 1/2 cup | Almond milk | 
- 1.5 scoops | Whey protein powder | 
- 1 tsp | Chia seeds | 
- 2 tbsp | Almonds or Mixed Nuts | 

## Directions

1. Mash half of banana in a medium jar or large glass;
2. chop other banana half and add to jar with remaining ingredients.
3. Stir, cover, and
4. refrigerate overnight;
5. serve (garnished with fresh banana, optional).
