## Ingredients

- 1 | Egg |
- 2 | Egg Whites |
- 1/4 cup | Milk |
- 1/2 tsp | Vanilla Extract |
- 1/2 tsp | Cinnamon |
- 1/8 tsp | Ground Nutmeg |
- 8 | Bread Slices, thick cut |

## Directions

1. Preheat skillet to 200/medium-high heat
2. Mix egg & egg whites until foamy, then add all ingredients except Bread.
3. Spray skillet and coat bread in mixture then add to skillet
4. Cook until golden brown on both sides, about 2 minutes each side.
