## Ingredients

- 3.5 tbsp | Quinoa/Buckwheat |
- ½ tbsp | Oil |
- ½ cup | Beans (Kidney/Navy/White) |
- 2 oz | Ground Turkey/Chicken |
- ⅔ cup | Diced Tomato/Diced Red Bell Pepper |
- 1 clove | minced garlic |
- Pinch | chili powder |
- Pinch | Dried Oregano |
- Pinch | S&P |

## Directions

1. Heat Oil Medium-Hi, add Garlic ~1 min
2. Add meat until brown
3. Add chopped veggies and seasonings
4. Add beans, grain, water & boil
5. Season with salt and simmer ~20 minutes
