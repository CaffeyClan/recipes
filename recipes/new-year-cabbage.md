## Ingredients

-  | 1 tbsp Olive Oil | 
-  | 1 tbsp Butter | 
-  | 1 Cabbage | 
-  | S&P to flavor | 
-  | 1 cup Chicken Broth | 

## Directions

1. Heat oil in pan on medium heat, add butter, cabbage, S&P
2. On low heat, let simmer for 5 minutes
3. Add broth, bring to boil then cover and simmer on medium-low for 15 minutes
4. Raise heat to medium-high, uncover and cook for 5 minutes
