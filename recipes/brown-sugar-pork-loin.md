## Ingredients

-  | 2 tsp S&P | 
-  | 1 tsp Cumin | 
-  | 1 tsp Chili Powder | 
-  | 1 tsp Cinnamon | 
-  | 3 tbsp Oil | 
-  | 1 cup Brown Sugar | 
-  | 2 tbsp Garlic, finely minced | 
-  | 1 tsp Hot Sauce | 
-  | Pork Loin | 

## Directions

1. Preheat oven 350
2. Add oil to skillet and heat to medium-high heat.
3. Mix S&P, cumin, chili, & cinnamon. Coat pork with it.
4. Add pork to skillet, browning all sides
5. Mix sugar, garlic, & hot sauce.
6. Once pork is browned on each side, remove from heat and coat with brown sugar mix.
7. Place pork in deep pan, to catch drippings.
8. Bake 25 minutes. Remove and rest for 10 minutes.
