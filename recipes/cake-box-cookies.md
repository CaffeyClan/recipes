## Ingredients

-  | 1 box Chocolate Cake Mix | 
-  | 1 cup Peanut Butter | 
-  | 2 Eggs | 
-  | 1/4 cup Oil | 
-  | 2 tbsp Water | 
-  | Powdered Sugar - topping | 

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 375
2. Mix all ingredients together
3. Drop ½ tbsp balls - whatever shape they are they resemble
4. Cook 9-11 minutes
5. Let cool
6. Sprinkle with Powdered Sugar
