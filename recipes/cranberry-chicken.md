## Ingredients

-  | Chicken (Whole, boned, or boneless) |
- 1 can | Whole Cranberry Sauce |
-  | Small bottle French Dressing (not oily type; creamy type) |
- 1 package | Onion Soup Mix |

## Directions

1. Preheat oven 350
2. Mix onion and dressing together
3. Wash and clean chicken, then place in baking pan
4. Pour dressing mix over chicken
5. Pour cranberry sauce over dressing mix
6. Cook 1 hour for whole chicken
7. Broil 3 minutes
