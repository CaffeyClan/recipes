## Ingredients

- 2 1/2 pounds | Chicken |
- 1/2 cup | Soy Sauce |
- 1/2 cup | Honey |
- 3 cloves | Garlic, whole |

## Directions

1. Cut chicken into bite-size pieces
2. Add all ingredients to crock pot
3. Cook low for 4 hours, stirring once halfway through
