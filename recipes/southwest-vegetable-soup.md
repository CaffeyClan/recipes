## Ingredients

-  | 1 pound Ground Beef | 
-  | 5 cup Water | 
-  | 1 can Mexican-style Tomatoes | 
-  | 1 can Black Beans, drained | 
-  | 1 can Kidney Beans, drained | 
-  | 1 can Whole Kernel Corn, drained | 
-  | 1 can Pinto Beans, drained | 
-  | 1 can Tomato Sauce | 
-  | 1 can Green Beans, drained | 
-  | 1 Chili Seasoning mix | 

## Directions

1. In large pot on medium-high heat, bring all ingredients to a boil.
2. Once boiling, turn back heat and simmer, stirring occasionally, for 2 hours
3. Serve with corn bread or rice.
