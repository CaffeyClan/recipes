## Ingredients

- 1 | med Whole Grain Tortilla |
- 2.5 oz | Ground Turkey |
- 1/2 small | Bell Pepper/Yellow Onion |
- 1 tsp | Oil |
- 4 tbsp | Black Beans/Kidney Beans |
- pinch | Chili powder |
- pinch | Paprika |
- pinch | Ground Cumin |
- pinch | Garlic Powder |
- pinch | Pepper |

## Directions

1. Add oil to med-hi heat in Skillet
2. Add meat, chop veg, seasoning
3. Cook while dicing meat up
4. Add ~2-3 tbsp water & beans
5. Simmer until thick
6. Wrap in tortilla and enjoy
