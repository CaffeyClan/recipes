## Ingredients

-  | 4 cups Chicken Broth | 
-  | 2 can Biscuits | 
-  | 1 cup Flour | 
-  | 2 cans Chicken, drained | 
-  | You can adjust the amount of biscuits and chicken to your liking | 

## Directions

1. In a large pot, add chicken broth and bring to boil
2. Quarter and flour biscuits
3. When broth boiling, bring heat to medium and add floured biscuits.
4. Cook for 5-10 minutes, then add chicken.
5. Cook another 5-10 minutes
