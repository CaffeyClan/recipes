## Ingredients

-  | 1 tsp Onion Powder | 
-  | 1 tsp Garlic Powder | 
-  | 1-3 tbsp Chili Powder | 
-  | 1 1/2 tsp Salt | 
-  | 4 tbsp Brown Sugar | 
-  | Pork Loin | 

## Directions

1. Mix seasoning in a large bag. Add pork to bag and let sit 15 minutes.
2. Grill on medium-high heat for 20 minutes, rotating every 5 minutes
3. OR
4. Bake at 350 for 25 minutes
