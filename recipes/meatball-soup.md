## Ingredients

- 5.5 oz | Ground Turkey |
- 1/2 cup | Red/Yellow Onion |
- 1/2 cup | Zucchini/Carrot/Cauliflower |
- 2 cup | Chicken/Vegetable Broth |
- 2 oz | Spagetti/Rice/Soba Noodles |
- Pinch | Paprika |
-  | S&P |

## Directions

1. Mix meat & diced onion, create 1 inch meatballs
2. In large saucepan add broth & veggies
3. Bring to boil on high eat
4. Add meatballs, reduce to medium-low heat. Cook ~25 minutes
5. Once meatballs are cooked, add pasta and cook until pasta is done
6. Top with desired herbs, enjoy!
