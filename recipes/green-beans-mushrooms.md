## Ingredients

-  | 2 tbsp Olive Oil | 
-  | 16–ounces Green Beans | 
-  | 16-ounces Button Mushrooms, sliced | 
-  | 2 Garlic Cloves, thinly sliced | 
-  | S&P | 
-  | 1 tsp Thyme Leaves, roughly chopped for topping | 

## Directions

1. Heat oil in large skillet over medium-high heat
2. Add green beads, cook covered until bright, ~5 minutes
3. Add mushrooms, cook covered an additional 5 minutes
4. Add garlic and S&P, cook uncovered for ~2 minutes
5. Remove from heat, sprinkle with thyme
