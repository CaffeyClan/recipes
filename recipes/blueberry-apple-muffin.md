## Ingredients

- 1 medium | Banana | 
- 1/2 cup | Apple puree | sugarless
- 6 | Eggs | 
- 3 scoops | Whey protein powder | 
- 1 cup | White self-rising flour | 
- 1 cup | Almond flour | 
- 1 1/2 cup | Blueberries | fresh is best

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 190 °C. Line a 12-hole muffin tray with paper cases.
2. Process banana and apple purée in a food processor until smooth. Add canola oil and eggs, pulse to combine.
3. In a medium bowl, stir flour mixture and banana mixture until combined. Fold in blueberries.
4. Divide mixture evenly among muffin cups. Bake ~15–18 minutes until a skewer comes out clean.
5. Let cool, store in airtight container (last 7 days) or freeze (last up to 3 months).
6. To Freeze: Wrap individually in plastic wrap.
7. To Reheat: Sit at room temp for 30-60 minutes OR bake 10-15 minutes at 350
8. For higher protein: Serve two muffins with a protein shake or Greek yogurt
