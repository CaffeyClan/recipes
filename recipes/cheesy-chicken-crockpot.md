## Ingredients

-  | 6 Chicken Breasts | 
-  | S&P, Garlic Powder to taste | 
-  | 2 can Cream of Chicken | 
-  | 1 can Cheddar Cheese soup | 
-  | Parsley, chopped *Optional | 
-  | Hot cooked Pasta | 

## Directions

1. Season chicken with S&P and garlic powder. Add chicken to crockpot.
2. Combine cream of chicken and cheddar cheese soups, then pour on top of chicken
3. Cover and cook low 6-8 hours
4. Serve on top of pasta, top with parslet
