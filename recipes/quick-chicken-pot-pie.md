## Ingredients

-  | 2 9-inch Pie Crusts | 
-  | 1 can Mix Veggies | 
-  | 2 cups Chicken, canned | 
-  | 1 can Cream of Chicken | 
-  | 1/4 cup Milk | 

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 350
2. In bowl, mix veggies, chicken, and cream of chicken
3. Slowly mix in milk, stirring and avoid making it runny
4. Pour mix into one pie crust, top with other pie crust, sealing edges and creating slits on top
5. Bake 30 minutes or until golden brown
