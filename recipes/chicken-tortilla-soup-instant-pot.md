## Ingredients

-  | 4 cups Chicken Broth | 
-  | 14-oz DIced Tomatoes | 
-  | 2 tsp Cumin | 
-  | 1 tsp Ground Coriander | 
-  | 1 tsp Paprika | 
-  | 1 tsp Oregano | 
-  | 1 tsp Garlic, minced | 
-  | 1 tsp Jalapeno, sliced | 
-  | Pinch Chili Powder | 
-  | Pinch Salt | 
-  | 1 pound Chicken Breasts | 
-  | 14-oz Black Beans, drained | 
-  | 12-oz Sweet Corn, drained | 

## Directions

1. Add all ingredients to instant pot in order, without stirring.
2. Cover and Seal instant pot. Cook high pressure for
3. 8 minutes. When done, let steam naturally release.
4. Remove chicken and shred, then return to pot.
5. Serve with rice, make quesadillas, eat alone, or more!
