## Ingredients

- ¾ cups | Asparagus/Mushrooms |
- 1 | cup, chopped Pumpkin/Broccoli |
- 3 tsp | Oil |
- 1.5 oz | Cheddar/Tofu |

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Place veggies in baking dish
3. Drizzle with Oil, S&P
4. Stir & Bake for 20-35 minutes; stir every 10 minutes
5. *Optional Lemon juice on top
