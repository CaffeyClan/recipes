## Ingredients

-  | 1 pkg, 16-oz Pound Cake Mix, plus ingredients to prepare mix | 
-  | 1/4 cup Brown Sugar | 
-  | 1 tbsp Flour | 
-  | 1/4 cup Chopped Nuts | 
-  | 1 tsp Ground Cinnamon | 
-  | Strawberries, blueberries, raspberries, and powdered sugar for topping | 

## Directions

1. Coat crockpot with nonstick spray
2. Prepare cake mix according to package
3. Add remaining ingredients, except berries, to cake mix
4. Pour batter into crockpot, cover and cook high 3-4 hours
5. Serve with berries and powdered sugar on top
