## Ingredients

-  | 1 tsp Vegetable Oil | 
-  | 2 tbsp Parmesan Cheese | 
-  | 1/2 tsp Salt | 
-  | 1/2 tsp Garlic Powder | 
-  | 1/2 tsp Paprika | 
-  | 1/4 tsp Black Pepper | 
-  | 2 pounds Red Potatoes, halved | 

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 400, coat pan with veg oil
2. Mix all ingredients together, put in pan
3. Cook 15-20 minutes, flip and repeat
4. I like doubling the seasoning!
