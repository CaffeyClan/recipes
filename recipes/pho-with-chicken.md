## Ingredients

- 1.5 | Chicken/Vegetable Broth |
- 2/3 cup | Green Beans/Snap Peas/Peas |
- 2 tsp | Vegetable/Canola/Olive Oil |
- 3.5 oz | Chicken Breast |
- 2 oz | Egg/Rice/Whole Grain Pasta Noodles |
-  | Garlic, 1 clove, minced |
- 1 tsp | Ginger |
- 1/4 tsp | Cinnamon |
- 1 tbsp | Basil |
-  | Lime, 1 slice |
-  | S&P |

## Directions

1. Heat oil in skillet on medium heat
2. Add seasonings and cook ~1 min.
3. Add broth and meat, bring to boil
4. Reduce heat, partially cover and simmer ~15 minutes
5. Cook noodles to package instructions, drain
6. Add veggies to broth, simmer ~3 minutes or veg soften
7. Remove from heat.
8. Ladle soup over noodles. Top with basil and/or lime
9. Enjoy!
