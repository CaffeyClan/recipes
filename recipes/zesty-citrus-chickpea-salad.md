## Ingredients

-  | 1 pound Chickpeas, cooked | 
-  | 2 tsp Lemon Zest | 
-  | 2 tsp Orange Zest | 
-  | 2 tsp Lime Zest | 
-  | 1/2 Yellow Bell Pepper, finely cubed | 
-  | 1/4 Red Bell Pepper, finely cubed | 
-  | 1/4 Red Onion, finely cubed | 
-  | 1/4 Green Apple, finely cubed | 
-  | 1/3 cup Olive Oil | 
-  | 1 tbsp Wine Vinegar | 
-  | S&P to taste | 

## Directions

1. Mix together chickpeas-apple
2. Drizzle with oil, vinegar, S&P
3. Mix and enjoy
4. Served chill
