## Ingredients

- 3 ounces | Orange Jell-o | 
- 1 cup | Water | boiling
- 1/2 cup | Water | cold
- 3.4 ounces | Vanilla pudding | 
- 4 ounces | Cream cheese | soften
- 8 ounces | Cool whip | container
- 2 1/2 cups | Minature marshmallows | 
- 22 ounces | Mandarin Oranges | drained

## Directions

1. In a large bowl add the jello powder and boiling water. Whisk it together. Then, add the cold water and stir it in.
2. Put it in the fridge to cool for about 20 minutes.
3. Stir in the pudding powder and refrigerate for another 20 minutes until it sets.
4. Add in the cream cheese and cool whip and stir to combine.
5. Then stir in the marshmallows and mandarin oranges.
6. Refrigerate for at least an hour. Serve chilled.
