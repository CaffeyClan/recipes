## Ingredients

-  | 3 cup Bisquick | 
-  | 1 pound Sausage (Jimmy Dean) | 
-  | 4 cup Cheddar Cheese, shredded | 
-  | 1/2 cup Parmesan Cheese, grated | 
-  | 1/2 cup Milk | 
-  | 1/2 tsp Rosemary Leaves, crushed | 
-  | 1 1/2 tsp Parsley, chopped | 

## Directions

1. Preheat oven 350
2. Mix all ingredients (Works great in Kitchen Aid)
3. Form 1 on balls
4. Add to baking sheet
5. Bake 20-25 minutes, until meat cooked
