## Ingredients

-  | 1/2 cup Italian Breadcrumbs | 
-  | 3 tbsp Parmesan Cheese, grated | 
-  | 1/4 tsp Pepper | 
-  | 3 cups Zucchini, thinly sliced | 
-  | 2 Egg Whites | 

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 450. Coat baking sheet with cooking spray.
2. Combine bread crumbs, cheese, and pepper in shallow dish
3. In separate dish, lightly beat egg whites
4. Dip zucchini in eggs, then coat in breadcrumbs mix.
5. Place on baking sheet
6. Bake 20 minutes, flipping halfway through.
