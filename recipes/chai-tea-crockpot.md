## Ingredients

- 2 quarts | Water |
- 8 bags | Black Tea |
- 3/4 cup | Sugar *adjustable |
- 16 whole | Cloves |
- 16 whole | Cardamom Seeds |
- 5 | Cinnamon Sticks |
- 8 slices | Ginger |
- 1 cup | Milk |

## Directions

1. Combine water-ginger in crockpot. Cover, cook high for 2 hours
2. Strain mixture, discard solids. This tea can be covered and refrigerated up to 3 days.
3. Stir in milk just before serving. Serve warm or chilled.
