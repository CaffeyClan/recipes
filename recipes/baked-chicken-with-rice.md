## Ingredients

- 4.5 oz | Chicken Breast |
- 2/3 cup | Cauliflower/Broccoli/Carrot |
- 1/2 cup | Zucchini/Bell Pepper |
- 4 tbsp | Brown/Basmati/Black Rice |
- 2 tsp | Olive/Sesame/Vegetable Oil |
-  | S&P |

## Directions

1. Cook rice according to package
2. Preheat oven to 425, season chicken with S&P
3. Add oil to baking dish, then chicken and veggies
4. Bake until chicken cooked, ~20 minutes
5. Add rice, chicken, and veggies to plate, Enjoy!
