## Ingredients

- 1 tsp | Olive/Canola Oil |
- 2 | Eggs |
- 1/2 | Zucchini/Yellow/Acorn Squash |
- 1.5 tbsp | Greek Yogurt |
- 1/2 cup | Whole Wheat/Buckwheat/Oat Flour |

## Directions

1. Grate the veggies
2. Mix veggies, flour, egg, and seasonings of choice in bowl
3. Heat skillet on med and add oil
4. Cook 'pancakes' ~2 min each side
5. Serve with yogurt and enjoy!
