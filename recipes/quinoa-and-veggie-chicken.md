## Ingredients

- 4 tbsp | Red/White/Rainbow Quinoa |
- 3 oz | Chicken Breast |
- 1/2 cup | Tomato, chopped |
- 1/2 cup | Green Beans/Peas/Corn |
- 1/2 tbsp | Olive/Canola/Sesame Oil |
- Pinch | Oregano |
- Pinch | Cumin |
- Pinch | Ground Coriander |
-  | S&P |

## Directions

1. Cook grain according to package
2. Heat oil in saucepan, add chopped meat and cook
3. Add veggies and seasonings. Simmer ~5 minutes
4. Combine grain and meat, enjoy!
