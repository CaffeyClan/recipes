## Ingredients

-  | 1 1/2 tbsp All-Purpose Flour | 
-  | 1 tsp Italian Seasoning | 
-  | 1/4 Salt x2 | 
-  | 1/4 cup Panko | 
-  | 1 tbsp Olive Oil | 
-  | 2 Egg Whites | 
-  | 2 Zucchini, cut into fry-size | 
-  | Lemon Wedges | 

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 425, line large baking sheet with parchment paper or nonstick foil.
2. In bowl, mix flour, italian seasoning, & salt.
3. In another bowl, mix bread crumbs and oil.
4. In third bowl, mix egg whites and salt, mix until peaks form
5. Dip zucchini in flour, then egg, then bread crumbs.
6. Arrange in single layer on sheet, spray with olive oil nonstick cooking spray
7. Bake about 12 minutes, flipping once. Serve with lemon wedges.
