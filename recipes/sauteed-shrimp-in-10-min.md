## Ingredients

-  | 2 pounds Shrimp, deveined & cleaned | 
-  | 1 tsp Ground Turmeric Powder | 
-  | 1 tsp Garlic Powder | 
-  | 1 tsp Sea Salt | 
-  | 1 tsp Black Pepper | 
-  | 1/2 tsp Paprika | 
-  | 1 tbsp Fresh Lemon Juice | 
-  | Oil or Butter for sauteing | 

## Directions

1. Have patted-dried shrimp in large bowl. Add seasonings (turmeric-paprika) and mix.
2. Heat oil/butter in skillet on medium heat. Add shrimp until even layer created, don’t overfill.
3. Cook 1-2 minutes per side, until cooked through.
4. Remove from heat.
5. Squeeze lemon over shrimp and toss to combine.
