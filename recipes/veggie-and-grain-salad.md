## Ingredients

- 3.5 tsp | White quinoa/Buckwheat/Black rice |
- 1.5 cup | Lettuce/Arugula |
- ½ cup | Pumpkin/Broccoli/Cauliflower |
- ½ tsp | Coconut oil/Canola oil/Olive oil |
- ½ cup | Green peas/White beans |
-  | S&P, 1 tsp Lemon juice, ⅓ Dried basil |

## Directions

1. Cook grains to package directions
2. Hear oil on medium heat saucepan
3. Add chopped veg, cook 5-6 min
4. Mix together, add seasoning
5. Enjoy
