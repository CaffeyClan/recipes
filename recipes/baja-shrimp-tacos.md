## Ingredients

-  | 1 tbsp Oil | 
-  | 2 pounds Shrimp | 
-  | 1 tsp Chili Powder | 
-  | 1 tsp Cumin | 
-  | 1 tsp S&P | 
-  | 1/3 cup Flour | 
-  | 1/2 cup Sour Cream | 
-  | 2 tbsp Chipotle Adobe Sauce | 
-  | 1 tbsp Lime Juice | 
-  | 1/4 tsp Salt | 
-  | Tortillas of choice | 
-  | Purple Cabbage, shredded | 
-  | Cilantro | 
-  | Avocado slices | 
-  | Cotija Cheese | 

## Directions

1. Heat pan on medium-high heat with oil.
2. Mix Shrimp-Flour in pan and cook through, ~6minutes
3. In separate bowl, mix sour cream-salt and set aside
4. Assemble on tortilla with desired sides and enjoy
