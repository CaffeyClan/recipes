## Ingredients

-  | 2 cups Frozen Shelled Edamame | 
-  | 1 avocado, mashed | 
-  | 1/4 cup Cilantro, chopped | 
-  | 2 Scallions, finely chopped | 
-  | 2 tbsp Lime Juice | 
-  | 1 Garlic Clove, minced | 
-  | 1/4 tsp Salt | 
-  | 1/4 tsp Red Pepper Flakes | 
-  | 4 Corn Tostadas | 
-  | 2 cups Romaine Lettuce, | 
-  | shredded | 
-  | 1/4 cup Red Onion, | 
-  | thinly sliced | 
-  | 1/4 cup Plain Fat-Free Yogurt | 
-  | Cracked Black Pepper | 

## Directions

1. Cook edamame to package. Drain and rinse under cool water.
2. Add edamame to bowl and crush with potato masher.
3. Add avocado - pepper flakes.
4. Spread edamame mix on tostada
5. Top with lettuce, tomatoes, onion, and yogurt. Sprinkle with black pepper
