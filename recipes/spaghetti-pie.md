## Ingredients

-  | 8-10 ounces Spaghetti, cooked | 
-  | 1/3 cup Parmesan Cheese | 
-  | 1 pound Ground Beef, cooked | 
-  | 2 cups Mozzarella, shredded | 
-  | 8 ounces Cottage Cheese | 
-  | 1/4 cup Butter | 
-  | 2 Eggs | 
-  | 1 can Pasta Sauce | 

## Directions

1. Preheat oven 350, grease pie pan
2. Add butter, parmesan, and eggs to cooked spaghetti. Be sure to not make scrambled eggs!
3. In separate bowl, combine meat and sauce
4. Form crust in pie pan with spaghetti mix.
5. Add cottage cheese on top of spaghetti (like the pizza sauce of a pizza)
6. Top with meat mix in middle
7. Cook 30-45 minutes
8. Add mozzarella on top. Cook additional 5 minutes.
