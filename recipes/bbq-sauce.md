## Ingredients

-  | 2 cups Ketchup | 
-  | 1/2 cup Water | 
-  | 1/3 cup Apple Cider Vinegar | 
-  | 1/3 cup Brown Sugar | 
-  | 2 tbsp Mustard | 
-  | 1 tbsp Onion Powder | 
-  | 1 tbsp Garlic Powder | 
-  | 1/2 tsp Cayenne Pepper | 
-  | 1/4 tsp Salt | 

## Directions

1. Mix all on low heat
2. Simmer for 20 minutes (should be thin)
3. Remove from heat & cool (20-30 minutes)
4. Add to cooked meat
5. Save in fridge for 1 week or Freezer for 3-4 months
