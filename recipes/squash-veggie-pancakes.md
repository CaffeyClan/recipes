## Ingredients

- 1 tsp | Vegetable/Olive oil |
- 2 | eggs/8 quail eggs |
- ½ | Squash Summer/Pumpkin/Butternut |
- 7 tbsp | Whole wheat flour |

## Directions

1. Grate squash into large bowl
2. Add flour, eggs, seasoning
3. Skillet on medium heat, drizzle with oil
4. Drop tbsp of batter, cook ~2 min each side
5. Enjoy!
