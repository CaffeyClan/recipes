## Ingredients

- 2-4 | Chicken Breasts |
- 1/2 cup | Italian Bread Crumbs |
- 1/4 cup | Parmesan Cheese |
- 1/4 tsp | S&P |
- 1 tbsp | Olive Oil |
- 1 | Egg |
-  | Mozzarella Cheese, sliced or shredded |
-  | Marinara Sauce |

## Directions

1. Spread oil in bottom of crock pot
2. Mix Bread, parmesan, and S&P together in bowl.
3. Beat egg in separate bowl.
4. Dip chicken in egg, then brad mixture, then place in crock pot
5. Layer chicken with mozzarella cheese. Once all chicken is in the crock pot, top with Marinara sauce.
6. Cook low 7 hours or high 4 hours
