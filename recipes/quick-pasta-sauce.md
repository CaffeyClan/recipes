## Ingredients

-  | 1 tbsp Olive Oil | 
-  | 1/4 cup Onion, minced | 
-  | 2 tbsp Garlic, minced | 
-  | 28 ounces Tomato, canned | 
-  | 1/2 tsp S&P | 
-  | 1/4 tsp Red Pepper Flakes | 
-  | 1 tsp Basil, dried and finely chopped | 

## Directions

1. In skillet, heat oil on medium heat
2. Add onion and garlic, cook ~2 minutes
3. Add remaining ingredients and stir
4. Simmer on low for 10 minutes
