## Ingredients

-  | Cinnamon Roll |
-  | French Toast |
- 2 | Cinnamon roll packages |
- 6 | Eggs |
- 1/2 cup | Milk |
- 2 tsp | Vanilla |
- 2 tsp | Cinnamon |
- 1 cup | Syrup |

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 375
2. Cut each cinnamon roll into fourths
3. Add cinnamon rolls to baking dish
4. Mix remaining ingredients, except syrup, in separate bowl and pour on top of cinnamon rolls
5. Add syrup on top
6. Bake at 375 for 25 minutes
7. Optional: Top with icing
