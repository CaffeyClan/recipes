## Ingredients

- 6 | Eggs |
- 1 package | Breakfast Sausage |
- 3 medium | Potatoes |
- 1 cup | Cheese |
-  | Flour Tortillas |

## Directions

1. Scramble eggs
2. Cook sausage and drain
3. Cook potatoes in microwave until soft, then dice
4. Assemble burritos
5. Storage: Wrap in plastic and store in freezer. Microwave until warm, 3-5 minutes.
