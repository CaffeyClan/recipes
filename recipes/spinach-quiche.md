## Ingredients

- 1 package | Frozen Chopped Spinach, thawed and squeeze dried |
- 8 ounces | Grated Swiss Cheese |
- 1/2 cup | Bisquick |
- 4 tbsp | Onion, Grated |
- 1/2 tsp | S&P |
- 1/4 tsp | Nutmeg |

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 350, grease baking dish
2. Mix all ingredients together and add to baking dish
3. Bake at 350 for about 30 minutes, until slightly brown
