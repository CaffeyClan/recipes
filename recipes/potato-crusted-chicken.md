## Ingredients

- 3/4 cup | Potato Flakes |
- 1/4 cup | Shredded Cheddar Cheese |
- 1 tsp | Dried Oregano |
- 1 1/4 pound | Chicken Breasts |
- 1/4 cup | Mayo |

## Directions

1. Preheat oven 400. On large baking sheet, spray cooking spray
2. Mix flakes, cheese, and oregano
3. Brush chicken with mayo, then coat with flake mix\
4. Place on sprayed baking sheet
5. Cook 25 minutes, until chicken cooked
