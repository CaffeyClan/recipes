## Ingredients

- 1 cup | Milk |
- 1/2 cup | Half & Half |
- 3 tbsp | Butter |
- 3 3/4 | Bread Flour |
- 3 tbsp | Sugar |
- 1 1/2 tsp | Salt |
- 1 1/2 tsp | Yeast |

## Directions

1. Add ingredients one at a time, ensuring room temperature for all ingredients (no cold milk/butter)
2. Bake as a Regular White
3. Cool, store for 4 days on counter, 3 months in Freezer
