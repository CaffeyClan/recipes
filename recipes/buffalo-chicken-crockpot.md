## Ingredients

- 2 pounds | Chicken |
- 1 cup | Hot Sauce |
- 1/3 cup | Coconut Oil |
- 3 tbsp | Soy Sauce |
- 1 1/2 tsp | Garlic Powder |
- 1/2 tsp | Cayenne *Makes Spicier |

## Directions

1. In saucepan, add everything but chicken. Cook on medium-high heat until coconut oil melted.
2. Add chicken to crock pot, pour saucepan over chicken
3. Cover and cook low for 4-6 hours
