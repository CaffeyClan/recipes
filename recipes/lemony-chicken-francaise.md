## Ingredients

- 4 sprays | Cooking spray | 
- 4 cutlets | Chicken breast | 
- 3/4 tsp | Salt | 
- 1/2 tsp | Garlic powder | 
- 1/4 tsp | Black pepper | 
- 1/4 cup | All-purpose flour | 
- 2 eggs | Eggs | 
- 2/3 cup | Chicken stock | 
- 1/3 cup | White wine | 
- 2 tbsp | Lemon juice | 
- 1, thinkly sliced | Lemon | 
- 1 tbsp | Butter | 
- 2 tbsp | Fresh parsley | 

## Directions

1. Place chicken between two sheets of plastic wrap and pound to a 1/4-inch thickness.
2. Arrange cutlets in a single layer and sprinkle with salt, garlic, and pepper. Sprinkle with flour and rub in.
3. Place whisked eggs into shallow dish, dip the chicken.
4. Coat large nonstick skillet with cooking spray and set to medium heat.
5. Add cutlets in a single layer, cook until lightly browned and cooked through.
6. Whisk stock, wine, lemon juice, and 1 tbsp flour. Add to skillet and bring to boil over medium heat stirring frequently.
7. Stir in pinch of salt and lemon slices, cook until thicken, about 3 minutes.
8. Stir in butter.
9. Add chicken to pan and coat in sauce.
10. Sprinkle with parsley.
