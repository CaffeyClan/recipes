## Ingredients

-  | 2 tbsp Butter | 
-  | 1 tbsp Sugar | 
-  | 1 tbsp Brown Sugar | 
-  | 1/4 tsp Vanilla Extract | 
-  | 5 tbsp Flour | 
-  | 1/4 tsp Baking Powder | 
-  | Dash Salt | 
-  | 2 tbsp Chocolate Chips | 

## Directions

1. Add butter only to mug of choice, microwave untitled melted
2. Add and mix remaining ingredients from top to bottom
3. Microwave for 1 minute
