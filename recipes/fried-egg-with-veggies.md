## Ingredients

- 2 | Eggs |
- ½ | Zucchini/Green Beans |
- ⅓ cup | Bell Pepper |
- 2 tsp | Butter |
- 2 slices | Bread, Whole Wheat/Buckwheat |

## Directions

1. Cook veggies in 1 tsp butter on med-hi until soft
2. Push veggies to side,
3. Add 1 tsp butter & egg on med-low
4. Serve with toast
