## Ingredients

- 4 sprays | Cooking spray | 
- 4 slices | Multigrain bread | 
- 4 eggs | Egg | 
- 1/8 tsp | Salt | 
- 1/2 cup | Cheddar cheese | 
- 1/8 tsp | Black pepper | 

## Directions

1. Place sheet pan in over. Preheat to 425°F.
2. Cut hole in center of each bread slice.
3. Remove pan from oven, coat with cooking spray.
4. Arrange bread slices and cutouts on pan, crack one egg into each bread slice.
5. Sprinkle salt over eggs, cheese over bread cutouts.
6. Bake 7-10 minutes, until egg whites are set.
7. Top with black pepper.
