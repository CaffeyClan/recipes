## Ingredients

-  | 1 Bell Pepper, dinced | 
-  | 1 Onion, diced | 
-  | 2 Carrots, diced | 
-  | 1 pound Ground Turkey | 
-  | 2 Egg Whites | 
-  | 1/4 cup Ketchup | 
-  | 1 tsp Dried Thyme | 
-  | 1/2 tsp Dried Sage | 
-  | 1/2 tsp S&P | 

## Directions

1. Preheat oven 425. Spray muffin pan with cooking spray
2. In skillet, saute onion, pepper, carrot for about 10 minutes.
3. Once veggies have cooked, place in fridge to cool ~10 minutes
4. Mix all ingredients together in large bowl (veggies, meat, and remaining items listed)
5. Add to muffin pan, fill muffin shape. *Place pan underneath muffin pan to catch drippings
6. Bake 20-25 minutes
