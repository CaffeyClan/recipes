## Ingredients

-  | 3 can Buttermilk Biscuits, quartered | 
-  | 1 cup Sugar | 
-  | 2 tsp Ground Cinnamon | 
-  | 1/2 cup Butter | 
-  | 1 cup Brown Sugar | 
-  | Chopped nut, raisins for topping | 

## Directions

1. Preheat 350, grease 9” circle pan
2. Mix sugar and cinnamon, coat quartered biscuits in mix. Add to circle pan.
3. On stove, melt butter in saucepan on medium-high heat
4. Add brown sugar, boil 1 minute then pour over biscuits
5. Bake 35 minutes, cool 10 minutes
