## Ingredients

-  | 2 Salmon fillets | 
-  | 1 tsp Paprika | 
-  | 1/2 tsp Garlic Powder | 
-  | 1/2 tsp Salt | 
-  | 1/2 tsp Black Pepper | 
-  | 1 tbsp Butter, melted | 

## Directions

1. Preheat oven 450
2. Line a baking sheet with foil and cooking spray
3. Put salmon on sheet, skin-side down
4. Brush with melted butter
5. Generously add seasonings
6. Bake for 12-16 minutes, or until cooked through
7. Serve with lemon slices and/or dill as garnish
