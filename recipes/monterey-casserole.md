## Ingredients

-  | 1 pound Hamburger Meat | 
-  | 1 can Cream of Chicken | 
-  | 1 can Ranch Style Beans | 
-  | 1 can Rotel | 
-  | Corn Tortillas, cut to 2” pieces | 
-  | Velveeta, cut to 1” cubes | 

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 350. Grease casserole dish.
2. In a bowl, mix meat-rotel
3. Layer:
4. Tortilla
5. Mixture
6. Velveeta
7. Bake 350 for about 30 minutes, or until cheese melted
