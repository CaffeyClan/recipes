## Ingredients

- 4 sprays | Cooking spray | 
- 1 pound | Chicken breast | 
- 1 tbsp | Balsamic vinegar | 
- 1 tbsp, chopped | Rosemary | 
- 1 tsp | Salt | 
- 1/2 tsp | Black pepper | 
- 2 cloves, minced | Garlic | 
- 1 1/2 pounds, peeled and cut to wedges | Yukon gold potatoes | 
- 2, cut to large chunks | Carrots | 
- 1 1/2 tbsp | Olive oil | 
- 1 1/3 cup | Frozen green peas | 
- 2 tbsp | Lemon juice | 

## Directions

1. Preheat 425°F, coat raosting pan with cooking spray.
2. In bowl, combine chicken, vinegar, rosemary, salt, pepper, and garlic. Let stand at roome temperature.
3. In roating pan, combine potatoes, carrot, and oil; toss to coat. Roast about 25 minutes, until browned.
4. Coat large skillet with cooking spray, heat pan to medium-high heat.
5. Add chicken to pan, smooth side down and cook until browned, about 4 minutes, on that one side.
6. Once roasting pan is done, stir vegetables and add chicken to pan, browned side up.
7. Add back to oven until chicken is cooked through, about 15 minutes.
8. Bring small saucepan of water t oboil, add peas and boil 2 minutes. Drain water, add salt, pepper, and lemon juice to peas. Garnish with rosemary.
