## Ingredients

- 4 | Chicken Breasts |
- 1 cup | Salsa |
- 1 | Taco Seasoning Package |
- 1 can | Cream of Chicken |
- 1/2 cup | Sour Cream |

## Directions

1. Coat chicken with Taco seasoning, then add to crockpot
2. In bowl, mix salsa and cream of chicken, then pour over chicken in crock pot
3. Cook low for 8 hours, or overnight.
4. Turn crock pot off, shred chicken, & stir in sour cream
5. Over a bed of rice, as quesadillas, or nacho topping!
