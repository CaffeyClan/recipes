## Ingredients

-  | 1/2 pound Italian Sausage | 
-  | 2 cups Water | 
-  | 1 cup uncooked Long Grain Rice | 
-  | 1 Onion, finely chopped | 
-  | 1 Green Bell Pepper, finely chopped | 
-  | 1/2 cup Celery, finely chopped | 
-  | 1 1/2 tsp Salt | 
-  | 1/2 tsp Ground Red Pepper | 

## Directions

1. Brown sausage in skillet for 8 minutes over medium-high heat, breaking up meat. Place into crockpot
2. Stir remaining ingredients into crockpot.
3. Cover and cook low 2 hours
