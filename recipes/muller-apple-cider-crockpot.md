## Ingredients

- 2 quarts | Apple Cider/Juice |
- 1/4 cup | Brown Sugar |
- 1 | Cheesecloth |
- 8 whole | Allspice Berries |
- 4 | Cinnamon Sticks, halved |
- 12 Whole | Cloves |
- 1 | Orange |
-  | Cinnamon Sticks *Optional |

## Directions

1. Combine apple cider/juice and brown sugar into crockpot.
2. Rinse cheesecloth, squeeze out water. Wrap allspice berries and cinnamon sticks, tie securely.
3. Stick cloves into orange and cut orange into quarters.
4. Add cheesecloth and oranges to crockpot. Cover and cook high 3 hours
5. Once cooked, can turn to low and kept warm for up to 3 hours. Ladle and enjoy with cinnamon sticks
