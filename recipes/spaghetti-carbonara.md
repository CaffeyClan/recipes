## Ingredients

-  | 8 ounces Spaghetti | 
-  | 2 Eggs | 
-  | 1/2 cup grated Parmesan | 
-  | 4 slices Bacon, diced | 
-  | 4 cloves Garlic, minced | 
-  | S&P to taste | 
-  | 2 tbsp Parsley, chopped | 

## Directions

1. Cook spaghetti to box directions
2. Whisk and set aside eggs & parmesan
3. Cook bacon
4. Add garlic to bacon when finished, 1 minute on low heat
5. Add cooked pasta & remaining ingredients
6. Add tbsp of pasta water as needed to create creamy consistency
