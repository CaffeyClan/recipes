## Ingredients

-  | 1 1/2 pound Ground Beef | 
-  | 1 package Onion Soup Mix | 
-  | 1 cup Ketchup | 
-  | 1/2 cup Water | 
-  | 1-2 cups fine Cracker Crumbs | 

## Directions

1. Preheat oven 350
2. Mix all ingredients together
3. Form into loaf, put into baking pan with sides to catch drippings.
4. Bake 1 hour, or until inside done.
