## Ingredients

-  | 1/2 cup Flour | 
-  | 1 tsp S&P | 
-  | 1 1/2 pound boneless Pork Chops | 
-  | 1/4 cup Vegetable Oil | 

## Directions

1. In skillet, heat oil on medium-high heat
2. Mix flour and S&P together in bowl
3. Trim and rinse pork chops, drying well
4. Coat pork chops in flour mix well
5. Fry pork chops ~1 minute each side, or until brown
