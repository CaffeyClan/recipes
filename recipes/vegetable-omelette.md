## Ingredients

- 3 | Eggs |
- 1/5 | Cucumber/Bell Pepper/Broccoli |
-  | Hanful Spinach/Kale/Leek |
- 2 slices | Whole Grain/Multigrain/Sourdough Bread |
- 1 tsp | Olive/ Canola/Butter |
-  | S&P |

## Directions

1. Dice veggies
2. In bowl, mix eggs and veggies with S&P
3. Heat oil in pan on medium-high heat
4. Add egg mix
5. When top of egg is thicken, fold egg in half and put on plate
6. Serve with bread
