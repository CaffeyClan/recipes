## Ingredients

-  | 1/2 pound Penne, cooked | 
-  | 4 tsp Olive Oil | 
-  | 1 pound Ground Turkey | 
-  | 1 Red Onion, finely chopped | 
-  | 3 Garlic Cloves, minced | 
-  | S&P | 
-  | 1 (9-ounce) package Frozen, thawed Artichoke Hearts | 
-  | 1/2 cup Basil, chopped | 
-  | 1/3 cup grated Pecorino or Parmesan | 

## Directions

1. Heat 2 tsps oil in skillet over medium-high heat.
2. Add turkey-S&P, using ½ of garlic,, breaking turkey apart and cooking through
3. Transfer cooked turkey to bowl
4. Add remaining 2 tsp oil on medium heat
5. Add artichokes and remaining ½ of garlic, to skillet.
6. Cover and cook for ~5 minutes, until artichoke cooked
7. Add pasta and 1/2 cup pasta water to skillet until heated
8. Add bowl of turkey mix to pasta and cook until 2 minutes
9. Remove from heat and stir in basil and cheese
