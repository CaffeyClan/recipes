## Ingredients

-  | 1 1/2 tbsp Olive Oil | 
-  | 3 pound Chuck Roast | 
-  | S&P | 
-  | 1 Yellow Onion, diced | 
-  | 5 cloves Garlic, minced | 
-  | 1 1/4 cup Beef Broth | 
-  | 2 tsp Worcestershire Sauce | 
-  | 1 tbsp Thyme, minced | 
-  | 1 tbsp Rosemary, minced | 
-  | 2 1/2 pounds Gold Potatoes | 
-  | 1 pound Carrots | 
-  | 2 1/2 tbsp Cornstarch* | 
-  | 2 tbsp Parsley, chopped | 

## Directions

1. Season roast with S&P, add 1 tbsp oil to medium-high heated pan and brown roast on both sides, ~5 minutes each. Then place in crockpot.
2. Add 1/2 tbsp oil to pan, cook onion, ~2 minutes. Add garlic to onion, ~30 seconds. Add to crockpot.
3. In pan, add broth, worcestershire, thyme, and rosemary. Scrape brown bits from bottom of pan. Heat to boil.
4. Add potatoes and carrots to crock pot.
5. Pour broth from pan on top.
6. Cook low 8-9 hours
7. *To thicken sauce (optional), add cornstarch to crock pot after it finishes cooking.
8. Top with parsley and enjoy!
