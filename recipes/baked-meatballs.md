## Ingredients

-  | 1 pound Ground Beef | 
-  | 1 package Onion Soup Mix | 
-  | 1 Egg | 
-  | 1 tbsp Milk | 
-  | Bread crumbs - to taste | 
-  | S&P | 

## Directions

1. Preheat oven 350
2. Mix all together, make 1 inch balls
3. Add to baking dish that has a lip to catch drippings
4. Bake for 30 minutes
