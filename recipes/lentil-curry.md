## Ingredients

- 5 tbsp | Red/Black/Green Lentils |
- 1 | Tomoato, canned |
- Handful | Arugula/Spinach/Baby Spinach |
- 1 tsp | Olive/Canola/Vegetable Oil |
- 3 tbsp | Cheddar/Parmesan/Blue Cheese |
-  | Garlic, 1 clove, minced |
- Pinch | Curry Powder |
- Pinch | Cinnamon |
-  | S&P |

## Directions

1. Heat oil in pan with lid on medium-high heat
2. Add garlic and seasonings
3. Add lentils, tomatoes, and water, simmering ~15 minutes
4. Once thickened, turn off heat, add greens
5. Top with S&P and cheese
