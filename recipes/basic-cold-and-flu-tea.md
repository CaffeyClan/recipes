## Description

Garlic can irritate the stomach. If you are drinking this often you may need to adjust the amount of garlic or eliminate it.

## Ingredients

## Directions

1. Bring 3-4 slices of fresh ginger (skin on) and one clove of chopped garlic to a boil and simmer for approximately 20 minutes
2. Use ginger water to steep tea (black, green, oolong or any herbal tea)
3. Add cayenne powder as hot as can be tolerated to induce sweating
4. Honey to taste
5. Add milk or dairy alternative (almond, soy or other)
6. Drink 3-4 cups per day
7. Drink before bed and bundle up to induce sweat
