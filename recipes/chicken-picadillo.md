## Ingredients

- 4 tsp | Canola oil | 
- 1 1/4 pounds | Ground chicken | 
- 1 medium | Onion | 
- 1 large, chopped | Poblano pepper | 
- 3 cloves, minced | Garlic | 
- 1 tbsp | Oregano | 
- 2 tsp | Ground cumin | 
- 1/2 tsp | Salt | 
- 1/4 tsp | Red pepper flakes | 
- 15 oz | Tomato sauce | 
- 1/3 cup | Golden seedless raisins | 
- 1/3 cup, chopped | Olives, green variety | 
- 3 tbsp | Cilantro | 
- 1 tbsp | Lime juice | 
-  | Tortillas, for serving | 
- Thinly sliced | Radish, for serving | 

## Directions

1. Heat 2 tsps oil in large skillet over medium-high heat.
2. Add chicken and cook, breaking up wiht wooden spoon, until browned, about 4 minutes.
3. Transfer to medium bowl.
4. Add remaining 2 tsps oil to skillet. Add onion and poblano and cook, stirring frequently, until vegetables are golden, about 6 minutes.
5. Add garlic, oregano, cumin, salt, and pepper and cook, stirring constantly, about 30 seconds.
6. Stir in tomato sauce, raisins, olives, and chicken. Reduce heat, cover and simmer 5 minutes.
7. Remove from heat, stir in cilantro and lime juice.
