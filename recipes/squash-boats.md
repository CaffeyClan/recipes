## Ingredients

- 1 | Green/Yellow Zucchini |
- 2 oz | Ground Turkey |
- 3 tbsp | Brown/Basmati Rice |
- 1/2 | Carrot/Bell Pepper |
- 1 | ttsp Olive/Vegetable Oil |
- Pinch | Paprika |
-  | S&P |

## Directions

1. Cook rice according to package, Preheat oven to 350
2. Make boat by slicing zucchini in half and removing center
3. Chop pulp from zucchini, save for later
4. Heat oil in saucepan on medium heat
5. Cook meat and veggies, including pulp, until browned
6. Stir in rice and seasonings, fill boats and bake 15 minutes
7. Enjoy!
