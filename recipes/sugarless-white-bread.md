## Ingredients

- 1 1/4 cup | Water |
- 2 tbsp | Vegetable Oil |
- 1 1/2 tsp | Salt |
- 4 cups | Bread Flour |
- 2 tsp | Active Dry Yeast |

## Directions

1. Add water, oil, and salt to bread machine pan
2. Add flour, then create center well and add yeast
3. Set machine to ‘basic’ or ‘white bread’ and cook
4. Once done, let cool before cutting
