## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup | Cauliflower/Broccoli/Brussel Sprouts |
- 1 tbsp | Flaxseed/Sesame Seeds |
- 3 tbsp | Lentils |
- 2 tsp | Oil |
- 1.5 tbsp | Parm Cheese/Cheddar/Fet |

## Directions

1. Cook lentils to pkg
2. Heat oil in skillet on high
3. Add cooked lentils with dash of S&P
4. Chop veggies, Add to lentils, cook ~5 min
5. Sprinkle cheese & seeds on top
