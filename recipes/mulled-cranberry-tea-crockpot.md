## Ingredients

- 2 | Tea Bags |
- 1 cup | Boiling Water |
-  | 48-ounces Cranberry Juice |
- 1/2 cup | Dried Cranberries *Optional |
- 1/3 cup | Sugar |
- 1 | Lemon, quartered |
- 4 | Cinnamon Sticks |
- 6 Whole | Cloves |
-  | Lemon Slices & Cinnamon Sticks for topping *Optional |

## Directions

1. Add tea and boiling water to crockpot, let sit for 5 minutes, then remove tea bags
2. Stir in juice - cloves, Cover and cook low for 2-3 hours OR high 1-2 hours
3. Remove lemons, cinnamon sticks, and cloves. Serve with desired toppings.
