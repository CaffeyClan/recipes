## Ingredients

-  | Meat: | 
-  | 4 Pork Chops | 
-  | 2 Eggs | 
-  | 1/2 cup Flour | 
-  | 1/4 Cup COLD Water | 
-  | S & P | 
-  | 2 cups Panko Bread Crumbs | 
-  | Oil to fry | 
-  | Curry: | 
-  | 1 Onion, Sliced | 
-  | 1 Carrot, Cubed | 
-  | 2 Potatoes, Cubed | 
-  | 1 tbsp Black Pepper | 
-  | 1 pack Curry Mix | 
-  | 3 cups Water | 

## Directions

1. Pork Katsu:
2. S&P both sides of pork.
3. In bowl, add eggs, flour, and water. Add more flour or water until a think but smooth consistency.
4. Coat the pork chops in the batter, then Panko. Make sure every part of pork chops is coated well.
5. In pan, add oil and heat. Once hot, add pork chops, frying about 4 minutes each side until golden brown.
6. Remove cooked pork chops and let rest on paper towel.
7. Curry:
8. In pot, drizzle oil and heat to medium-high
9. Add onion, saute until translucent (~5 minutes)
10. Add vegetables and saute ~2 minutes
11. Add 3 cups of water and simmer for 10 minutes on low heat.
12. Add broken curry mix and stir thoroughly.
13. Add Pepper and simmer another 10-15 minutes on low heat.
