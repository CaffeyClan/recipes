## Ingredients

-  | 1/2 cup Butter, softened | 
-  | 1/2 cup Sugar | 
-  | 1 Egg, lightly beaten | 
-  | 1 cup Molasses | 
-  | 2 1/2 cups Flour | 
-  | 1 1/2 tsp Baking Soda | 
-  | 1 tsp Ground Cinnamon | 
-  | 2 tsp Ground Ginger | 
-  | 1/2 tsp Ground Cloves | 
-  | 1/2 tsp Salt | 
-  | 1 cup Hot Water | 
-  | Whipped cream, orange peel *Optional, for topping | 

## Directions

1. Beat butter and sugar, add one at a time egg-salt ingredients.
2. Stir in hot water and mix well
3. Coat crockpot with nonstick spray. Pour batter in.
4. Cover and cook high 3-4 hours
5. Serve with toppings
