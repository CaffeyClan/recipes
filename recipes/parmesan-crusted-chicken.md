## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup | Mayo |
- 1/4 cup | Parmesan Cheese |
- 4 | Chicken Breasts |
- 4 tsp | Italian Bread Crumbs |

## Directions

1. Preheat oven 425
2. Mix mayo and cheese together in bowl
3. Place chicken on baking sheet. Coat top with mayo mix
4. Sprinkle bread crumbs on top of chicken
5. Bake 20 minutes or until chicken cooked
