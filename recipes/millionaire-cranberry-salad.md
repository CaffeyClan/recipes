## Ingredients

- 2 cups | Sour cream | 
- 2 cups | Pineapple tidbits | well drained
- 2 cups | Minature marshmallows | 
- 1 cup | Pecans | chopped
- 2 cups | Coconut flakes | 
- 3 cups | Cranberries | fresh
- 3/4 cup | Sugar | 
- 1 cup | Water | 
- 1 stick | Cinnamon stick | optional

## Directions

1. Combine cranberries, sugar, water and cinnamon stick in a saucepan. Bring to a boil, reduce heat and let simmer 10 minutes. Cool completely (sauce will thicken upon cooling).
2. Add 1 ½ cups cooled cranberry mixture from step 1 with remaining ingredients and stir well.
3. Let sit 4 hours or overnight.
