## Ingredients

-  | 1/4 cup Ketchup | 
-  | 2 tbsp Apple Cider Vinegar | 
-  | 1 1/2 tbsp Brown Sugar | 
-  | 2 Garlic Cloves, minced | 
-  | 1 1/2 tsp Paprika | 
-  | 1 1/2 tsp Ground Cumin | 
-  | S&P | 
-  | 6 Chicken Breasts | 

## Directions

1. Spray grill, preheat to medium fire
2. In small bowl, make glaze by mixing ketchup-garlic.
3. In separate bowl or bag, add chicken and paprika-S&P
4. Add chicken to grill, turning often until cooked (~10 minutes).
5. Brush glaze on chicken and keep on heat an additional one minute, then remove from heat.
