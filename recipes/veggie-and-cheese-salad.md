## Ingredients

- 1.5 cup | Lettuce/Spinach/Arugula |
- 1 cup | Tomato, diced |
- 1 cup | Radish/Cucumber/Bell Pepper, diced |
- 1.5 tbsp | Honey Mustard Dressing/Pesto/Olive Oil |
- 1/3 cup | Feta/Blue/Halloumi Cheese |

## Directions

1. Chop veggies
2. *Halloumi: slice thinly,fry cheese with some oil on both sides
3. Combine all ingredients, topping with cheese of choice
4. Enjoy!
