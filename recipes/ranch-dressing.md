## Ingredients

-  | 1 cup Mayo | 
-  | 1/2 cup Sour Cream | 
-  | 2 tsp Lemon Juice | 
-  | 2 tsp Dried Parsley | 
-  | 1 tsp Dried Dill | 
-  | 1 tsp Dried Chives | 
-  | 1/2 tsp Garlic Powder | 
-  | 1/2 tsp Onion Powder | 
-  | 1/2 tsp Sea Salt | 
-  | 1/4 tsp Black Pepper | 
-  | 1/4 cup Unsweetened Almond Milk | 

## Directions

1. Whisk mayo-pepper together.
2. Add milk last, gradually until desired consistency
3. Refrigerate for at least 1 hour.
4. Store in fridge for up to 10 days
