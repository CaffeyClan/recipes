## Ingredients

-  | 2 tbsp Vegetable Oil | 
-  | 1 cup Rice | 
-  | 8-ounces Tomato Sauce | 
-  | 16 ounces Water | 
-  | 1 tsp Chili Powder* | 
-  | 2 tsp Tomato Bouillon* | 
-  | 2 tsp Garlic, minced | 

## Directions

1. In saucepan, heat oil on medium-high heat. Add rice and brown.
2. Add remaining ingredients in slowing and stir well
3. Boil. Once boiling, bring to low heat and simmer 20 minutes with lid on
4. Remove from heat, rest 5-10 minutes covered
5. Fluff and eat
6. *Cut back for less spice. I do 1/2 chili powder and 1/2 - 1 tsp tomato bouillon.
