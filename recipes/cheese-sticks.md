## Ingredients

-  | ⅔ cup Flour | 
-  | 1/3 cup Cornstarch | 
-  | 2 Eggs | 
-  | 1/4 cup Water | 
-  | 1 1/2 cup Italian Breadcrumbs | 
-  | 1/2 tsp Garlic Salt | 
-  | 1 tsp Italian Spices | 
-  | Mozzarella cheese in stick form (Can be block that is cut or | 
-  | premade cheese sticks.) | 

## Directions

1. Heat fryer to 350
2. Mix flour and cornstarch in one bowl
3. Mix eggs and water in another bowl
4. Mix breadcrumbs, garlic, and spices in third bowl.
5. Dip cheese in flour mix, egg mix, then crumb mix
6. Fry until golden brown
