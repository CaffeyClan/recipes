## Ingredients

-  | 1/2 tsp Cayenne Pepper | 
-  | 1/2 tsp Ground Ginger | 
-  | 2 tbsp Honey | 
-  | 2 tbsp Apple Cider Vinegar | 
-  | 2 tbsp Fresh Lemon Juice | 
-  | 1/2 cup Water | 

## Directions

1. Add all ingredients to jar with lid.
2. Tighten lid and shake jar to mix well
3. Store with lid on in refrigerator
4. Shake well before taking
5. 1 teaspoon to soothe sore throats and coughs
6. Repeat every couple hours, as needed
7. *NO honey for children under 1 years old
