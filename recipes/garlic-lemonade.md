## Description

This is another base recipe to drink daily through the duration of illness.

## Ingredients

- 2-4 | chopped garlic cloves |
- 3-4 slices | fresh ginger |
-  | Raw honey |
-  | Water |
- 2-3 | lemons |

## Directions

1. In a 1 quart mason jar combine 2-4 cloves chopped garlic and 3-4 slices of fresh ginger.
2. Cover and let steep for at least 20 minutes.
3. Add juice of 2-3 lemons and honey to taste (approximately ¼ cup)
4. You can strain or leave herbs in mixture.
5. Consume warm and as needed. It can be consumed at room temperature, but not chilled.
