## Ingredients

-  | 1 pound Ground Beef | 
-  | 6 oz Tomato Paste | 
-  | 1 tsp Salt | 
-  | 1/2 tsp Oregano | 
-  | 8 oz Mozzarella | 
-  | 2 cloves Garlic, minced | 
-  | 1 can Tomatoes | 
-  | 3/4 tsp Black Pepper | 
-  | 8 oz Lasagna noodles | 
-  | 24 oz Cottage Cheese | 

## Directions

1. Present oven 350
2. Brown & drain beef. Cook noodles according to package.
3. Add tomato paste - black pepper to beef, stir.
4. Cover and simmer for 20 minutes
5. In baking pan, layer: meat, noodle, cottage & mozzarella cheese
6. Top with parmesan cheese
7. Bake 45 minutes
