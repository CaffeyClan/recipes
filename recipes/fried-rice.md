## Ingredients

-  | 3 cups cooked Rice | 
-  | 2 tbsp Sesame Oil | 
-  | 1 Onion, chopped | 
-  | 1 cup Peas & Carrots | 
-  | 2-3 tbsp Soy Sauce | 
-  | 2 Eggs | 
-  | 2 tbsp Green Onions | 

## Directions

1. In pan, on medium heat add the Oil and Onion, cook until transparent.
2. Move onion to side, add peas & carrorts, soy sauce and cook until heated.
3. Move all items in pan to side, add eggs and cook through
4. Turn off heat and top with green onion
