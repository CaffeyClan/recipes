## Ingredients

-  | 4 cups Chicken Broth | 
-  | 1 can Nacho Cheese | 
-  | 1 can Rotel | 
-  | ½ pound Mozzarella | 
-  | Pinch Garlic Powder | 
-  | 1 can Cream of Chicken | 
-  | 1 small can Pet Milk | 
-  | 1 pound Velveeta | 
-  | 1 tsp Cumin | 
-  | Chicken, cooked, desired amount | 

## Directions

1. In large pot, bring broth to boil
2. Add ingredients one at time, mixing well and returning to boil after each
3. Simmer ~25 minutes, until all ingredients mixed
4. Serve with Red or Blue Doritos
