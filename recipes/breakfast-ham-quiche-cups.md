## Ingredients

- 5 | Eggs |
- 1 cup | Milk |
- 1 1/2 cup | Shredded Swiss Cheese |
- 1/4 cup | Green Onions, chopped |
- 1/2 tsp | Dried Thyme |
- 1/4 tsp | S&P |
- 18 slices | Ham |

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 350, spray muffin pan
2. Mix all ingredients but ham in separate bowl
3. Form ham in muffin pan to create ‘bowl’ shape
4. Add egg mixture, 3/4 full, ~3 tbsp of mixture
5. Bake 350 for 25 minutes
6. Storage: Cool, then freeze. Warm by microwaving about
7. 1 minute
