## Ingredients

- 2 cans | Chicken |
- 1 stick | Butter |
- 1 | Onion, finely diced |
- 1 package | Frozen Chopped Broccoli |
- 1 can | Cream of Mushroom |
- 1 can | Pet Milk |
- 8 ounces | Velveeta |
- 1 cup | Minute Rice |

## Directions

1. Preheat oven 350
2. Saute onion, broccoli, butter on medium-high heat in skillet. Once tender, add chicken until heated.
3. In casserole dish, add soup, milk, cheese and stir well. Microwave until cheese melts, about 7 minutes
4. Add onion mix and rice to casserole, stir well
5. Bake 30 minutes
