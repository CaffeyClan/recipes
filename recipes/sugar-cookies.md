## Ingredients

-  | 1 cup Butter, softened | 
-  | 1 cup Sugar | 
-  | 1 Egg | 
-  | 1 tsp Vanilla | 
-  | 2 tsp Baking Powder | 
-  | 2 3/4 cup Flour | 

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 400
2. In large bowl, cream butter and sugar with mixer
3. Add egg, mix
4. Add vanilla, mix
5. In separate bowl, mix baking powder and flour
6. Slowly add dry mix to wet mix
7. Roll on floured surface, making dough even and thin
8. Cut into shapes.
9. Bake for 7-10 minutes
