## Ingredients

-  | 2 tsp Canola Oil | 
-  | 1 Red Bell Pepper, diced | 
-  | 1 Carrot, diced | 
-  | 2 Garlic Cloves, minced | 
-  | 1 tbsp Ginger, minced | 
-  | 1 pound Ground Turkey | 
-  | 4 Scallions, thinly sliced | 
-  | 1 cup Frozen Peas, thawed | 
-  | 2 tbsp Soy Sauce | 
-  | 2 tsp Sesame Oil | 
-  | 1/4 tsp Salt | 
-  | 1/8 tsp Red Pepper Flakes | 
-  | 1/4 cup Cilantro, chopped | 
-  | 1 1/3 cups Rice, cooked | 
-  | 1 tbsp Sesame Seeds, toasted | 

## Directions

1. Heat oil in large skillet over medium-high heat. Add pepper & carrot for ~3 minutes.
2. Add garlic & ginger, cook ~30 seconds.
3. Add turkey and cook through, ~5 minutes.
4. Add scallions, cook ~1 minute.
5. Stir in peas-pepper flakes, cook ~2minues.
6. Remove from heat and stir in cilantro.
7. Serve with rice, top with sesame seeds.
