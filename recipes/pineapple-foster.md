## Ingredients

-  | 1 tsp Butter | 
-  | 1 1/2 cup Pineapple, cubed | 
-  | 4 tsp Dark Rum | 
-  | 4 tsp Brown Sugar | 
-  | 1/4 tsp Cinnamon | 
-  | 1 tbsp Walnuts, chopped & toasted | 

## Directions

1. Melt butter in medium skillet over medium heat.
2. Add pineapple until browned, ~8 minutes
3. Add rum, brown sugar, and cinnamon, cook until sugar dissolved and mixture syrupy, ~2 minutes
4. Add to ice cream, pancakes, or other dishes as a yummy topping!
