## Ingredients

-  | 1 cup Corn | 
-  | 1 cup Black Beans | 
-  | 1/2 Red Pepper, finely diced | 
-  | 1/4 cup Jalapeno Pepper, finely diced | 
-  | 1/4 cup Green Onions, chopped | 
-  | 2 cups Chicken, shredded | 
-  | 3 cups Monterey Jack, shredded | 
-  | 1 tsp Paprika | 
-  | 1/2 tsp Ground Cumin | 
-  | 1 tsp Chili Powder | 
-  | 1/2 tsp Salt | 
-  | 1 Egg | 
-  | 1 tbsp Water | 
-  | 12 Egg Roll Wrappers | 
-  | Vegetable Oil | 
-  | Zesty Dip: | 
-  | 1 cup Sour Cream or Greek Yogurt | 
-  | 1/2 cup Ranch Dressing | 
-  | 1 tbsp Taco Seasoning | 

## Directions

1. In a large bowl, mix Corn-Salt
2. In a small bowl, whisk egg and water together
3. Fill the Egg Roll Wrapper with mixture, then brush the edges with the egg wash mixture and fold.
4. Lightly brush oil on top of each egg roll
5. Air fry at 375 for 5 minutes. Flip and repeat.
6. Zesty Dip:
7. Mix ingredients, store in fridge.
8. Storage:
9. Fridge: Air-tight container for 4 days
10. Freezer: Cool completely, cover with plastic wrap and freeze for 4 hours. Transfer to freezer bag. Keeps for 6 months. Thaw before reheating.
11. Reheat at 350 for 5 minutes, flipping and repeating.
