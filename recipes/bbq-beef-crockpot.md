## Ingredients

-  | 3 pound Boneless Chuck Roast | 
-  | 1 tsp Garlic Powder | 
-  | 1 tsp Onion Powder | 
-  | S&P | 
-  | 18-ounces BBQ sauce | 

## Directions

1. Add all ingredients to crock pot
2. Cook low 6-8 hours
3. Shred, cook 1 more hour
4. Great as burger, or with mashed potatoes and green beans.
