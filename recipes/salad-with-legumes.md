## Ingredients

- ⅕ cups | Lettuce/Arugula |
- 1 tbsp | Honey mustard/Olive oil |
- 1 cup | Radish/Cucumber |
- ⅔ cup | Green peas/Chickpeas/Kidney beans |

## Directions

1. Slice veg to bite-size pieces
2. Toss ingredients together
3. Optional: Add pinch of S&P, 1 tsp lemon juice
4. Enjoy!
