## Ingredients

- 1 | Corn/Whole Grain/Low-Carb Tortilla |
- 1/2 tbsp | Olive/Vegetable Oil |
- 2 tbsp | Pesto/Sweet Chili/ Tahini sauce |
- 5 tbsp | Parmesan/Cheddar/Mozzarella |

## Directions

1. Spread sauce over tortilla
2. Add cheese and fold in half
3. Cook tortilla until crispy on both sides, enjoy!
