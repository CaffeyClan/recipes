## Ingredients

-  | 1/2 cup All-Purpose Flour | 
-  | S&P | 
-  | 4-6 Chicken Breasts | 
-  | 1/4 cup Butter | 
-  | 2 cans Cream of Mushroom | 
-  | 1 package Stuffing Mix | 

## Directions

1. Combine flour and S&P to large bag. Add chicken and shake to coat.
2. Melt butter in skillet over medium-low heat, brown chicken on both sides. Add to crockpot, pour soup cans on top
3. Prepare stuffing according to package, decreasing liquids by half. Add on top of chicken
4. Cover and cook high 3-4 hours.
