## Ingredients

- 6 ounces | Chicken | 
- 1 medium | Potato | 
- 3 tsp | Oil | 
- 1/2 | Onion | 
- 2/3 cups | Can tomatoes | 
- 1/2 cup | Water | 
- 1 clove | Garlic | 
- 1 pinch | Ground ginger | 
- 1 tsp | Curry powder | 

## Directions

1. Heat oil in pan, add onion
2. Add ginger and garlic
3. Add chicken until cooked
4. Add potatoes and S&P
5. Add tomatoes and water
6. Simmer ~20 minutes (potatoes soft)
