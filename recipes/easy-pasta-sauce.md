## Ingredients

-  | 2 tbsp Olive Oil | 
-  | 1 Onion, finely diced | 
-  | 3 cloves Garlic, minced | 
-  | 2 tsp Basil | 
-  | Pinch Red Pepper Flakes | 
-  | 1/2 tsp Salt | 
-  | 1/2 tsp Sugar | 
-  | 2 tsp Butter | 
-  | 28-ounce can Crushed Tomato | 
-  | 1/4 cup Water | 

## Directions

1. Heat oil over high heat. Saute onions until soften, ~3 minutes. Add garlic, cook 2 minutes.
2. Add basil - sugar, stir.
3. Add butter, stir and cook 1 minutes.
4. Add half of the can of tomatoes, reduce heat to low and stir well, scraping bottom on pan. Add remaining can.
5. Stir in water. If too thick, add additional water.
6. Simmer 10 minutes-1 hour.
